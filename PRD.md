# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PDR)
## BU EASYPAY: DIGITAL PAYMENT AND SHOP DISCOVERY PLATFORM FOR STUDENTS

### MANAGEMENT SUMMARY
   BU EASYPAY is a proposed platform intended to help students discover shops operating on or around their campus and access clear, verified information about those businesses and the capability is to initiate a payment to a participating merchant through an approved payment provider.

The problem to investigate is whether students have difficulty finding particular campus businesses, identifying the correct merchant business details, or confirming payments. The team must establish which of these problems is real, how often it occurs, who experiences it, and whether existing methods are inadequate.

The first version should address only the most important problem supported by research. Payment functionality should be included only if user research, feasibility checks, and payment-provider requirements support it.

### PRODUCT OVERVIEW
BU EASYPAY is a digital payment and shop discovery platform designed to make transactions between students and businesses or entrepreneurs on campus easier, faster and more convenient.

The platform will allow students to discover shops around campus, search for businesses by name or category, and make payments to registerd merchants using their bank accounts.

Instead of the stress of new students having to search physically for shops or struggle to find the correct bank details when making payments, BU EASYPAY provides a centralized businesses and conveniently pay them. This platform also provides shop owners with an opourtunity to register their businesses, receive digital payments and reach more potential customers.

### PROBLEM STATEMENT
Students frequently purchase items and services from shops within the campus,and bank transfer is the most commonly used payment method. However several challenges make these transactions inconvenient. 
These challenges include;

1. Students find it inconvenient to locate particular shops around campus especially the 100 level students. 

2. Students also experience uncertainty when paying a shop by bank transfer which would lead multiple clarification by the merchant, especially when the verified merchant details are improperly stored.

3. Poor Online visibility for small businesses on the campus.

4. Delays and clear confusion (Mostly for incoming students) when confirming bank transfer.

5. Difficulity in the efficiency of obtaining the correct payment details of shop owners.

BU EASYPAY aims to address these problems by combining a shop discovery system with a very convenient digital payment solution.

Merchants around the campus don't have any online visibility bringing students to their place of work, BU EASYPY also addresses this by directing more students their place of work through our shop discovery feature

### TARGET USERS

+ STUDENTS: Those who need a reliable way to find food vendors, stationary shops, superstores, printing shops, salons and other businesses and an efficient way to pay for these certain services that allow for effective seperation of school and personal allowances.

+ SHOP OWNERS: Those looking for more business expansion in a way to get more students to find their business and a clearer way to share payment information.

### COPING METHODS AND WHY THE EXISTING SOLUTION FALL SHORT
Through our survey we discovered the following ways students cope with the problems we observed;

Question- How do students find a shop they have never visited?
+ Most incoming students find shops by asking students already familiar with the campus and those who are familiar took them a long time to get know the grounds of the campus.
  
Question- How do they obtain a shop owner's bank details? 
+ The students obtain a shop owner's bank details by reading it of a piece of paper which can lead to mistakes by human error.

Question- How do they confirm that a transfer reached the correct shop?
+ The way they confirm is by showing the shop owner a receipt of the purchase which could be fake.

Question- How do shop owners attract or inform students?
+ From our findings there hasn't been a way to pull in students except informing passersby what they do.

Question- Which existing apps, directories, groups, or social networks are used?
+ There was once a BUSA app but is no longer in use by the students because of the complexity of the app and lack of advertisment to incoming students.
 
### PROPOSED SOLUTION
BU EASYPAY is proposed as a searchable directory of participating campus businesses. Students would be able to search by shop name, category and view useful information about a business.
Also providing a centralized digital payment platform that connects students with campus businesses, making buying and selling easier, faster and more convenient solving delays associated with bank transfers and keeps track of payment.

### THE FIRST VERSION (MVP)
This is the smallest version that tests the most important problem established by the research conducted.The features include;
1. Student account creation and login.
2. Merchant application and shop registration.
3. Shop search by name and categories.
4. Basic transaction status and history.
5. Payment Integration.
6. Shop profile page.
7. Shop search engine.
8. Student and merchant dashboard.

### USER STORIES
STUDENTS:
+ As a student, I want to search for a shop by name so that i can find it easily.
+ As a student, I want to view shop information so that i know where i am paying.
+ As a student, I want to pay using my bank account so that i do not always need cash.
+ As a student, I want to view my transaction history so that i can monitor my payments.

MERCHANT:

### SCOPE OR PRIORITIES
 BU EASYPAY is a student payment platform that allows students to pay registered campus shops directly using their bank accounts. it also provides a search feature that helps students find shop and view available products or service.

 The platform will initially focus on registered campus businesses and student payments.
 #### PRIORITIES
 1. Easy Payments: Allow students to make payments directly from their bank account.
 2. Shop Search: Enable students to search for shops by name.
 3. Shop Registration: Allow campus businesses to register on the platform.
 4. Transaction History: Allow students to view their previous payments.
 5. Payment Security: Ensure payments are safe and properly confirmed.
 6. User-Friendly Design: Make the platform simple and easy for students and shop owners to use.

### ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
1. User Registration: Student must be able to create an account and log in successsfully.
2. Shop Registration: Shop owners must be able to register their businesses on the platform.
3. Shop Search: Students must be able to search for registered campus shops by name.
4. Bank Payments: Students must be able to make payments to registered shops using their bank accounts.
5. Payment Confirmation: Both students and shop owners must receive confirmation when a payment is successful.
6. Transaction History: Students must be able to view their previous transactions.
7. Payment Security: Users' personal and payment information must be protected.
8. Error Handling: The platform must display clear messages when a payment fails or an error occurs.

### ASSUMPTIONS
BU EASYPAY assumes that most students have access to a bank account and a smartphone with internet access. it also assumes that campus shop owners are willing to register their businesses on the platform. Students should be able to make payments without needing physical cash, while shop owners should be able to receive payments directly into their bank accounts.
